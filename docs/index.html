Frequently asked

The quick answers.

Anything else, call us on 0860 203 203 or email help@projecthelp.co.za.

Is the app really free for Auto Pedigree customers?
Yes. Your Auto Plus Plan covers the full Premium Help service for the agreed cover period at no additional cost to you. Download the app and use it as much — or as little — as you need.
How fast is armed response?
Average dispatch is under 60 seconds nationwide. Actual arrival time depends on traffic and the closest available unit — but with 5,500+ guards across the country, there's almost always someone close.
Does Project Help track my location?
No. Your GPS is shared only at the moment you press the panic button — and only with the dispatched guard. We don't log, sell or retain your movement data. Privacy is built in.
Can my family use the same plan?
Yes. Family cover is R23 a week for five people — you plus four family members — which works out to 66c per person per day. Everyone gets the full service: armed response, medical emergency, licence disc renewal, traffic fines, RAF claim assistance and Free Wills. Add it in the app, or call us on 0860 203 203.
What happens if I'm in a car accident?
Open the app, tap RAF Claim, and follow the steps: capture the scene, log the other parties, and generate the official accident report. Project Help then lodges and manages your Road Accident Fund claim end-to-end — at no extra cost.
I lost my phone. How do I re-link the app?
Just reinstall the app on the new phone and enter the same mobile number you registered at the dealership. Your Auto Plus Plan re-links automatically.
